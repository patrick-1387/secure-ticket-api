Scoring Rubric Guidance

Use this rubric to apply consistent scoring across intake requests.

1. Business Value

Measures expected impact on cost, risk reduction, revenue, quality, or cycle time.

ScoreDefinition
0No clear business value or value is speculative. Mostly a "nice to have."
1Very limited value. Small improvement for a narrow task with little measurable business impact.
2Some value. Improves efficiency or quality, but benefits are modest or hard to quantify.
3Moderate value. Clear benefit to cost, risk, revenue, quality, or speed for a meaningful process.
4High value. Strong measurable impact on a priority business outcome, with credible benefit estimates.
5Transformational value. Material enterprise impact, major savings/revenue/risk reduction, or strategic advantage.
2. Reach

Measures how many people, teams, customers, processes, or transactions are impacted.

ScoreDefinition
0No identifiable user group or process impacted.
1Impacts one individual, niche role, or very small process.
2Impacts a small team or localized process.
3Impacts multiple teams, functions, or a recurring business process.
4Impacts a large function, region, customer segment, or high-volume process.
5Broad enterprise impact across multiple functions, regions, products, or customer groups.
3. Feasibility

Measures how realistic delivery is based on resources, dependencies, and readiness.

ScoreDefinition
0Not feasible with current capabilities, data, funding, or constraints.
1Very difficult. Major blockers, unclear ownership, or heavy dependencies.
2Challenging. Requires significant effort or coordination.
3Reasonably feasible with manageable dependencies.
4Highly feasible with available tools and clear ownership.
5Very easy to execute with low effort and proven approach.
4. Reuse Potential

Measures scalability and reusability of the solution.

ScoreDefinition
0One-off use case with no reuse.
1Very limited reuse, highly customized.
2Some reuse with meaningful rework.
3Reusable with moderate adaptation.
4Strong reuse potential across teams.
5Enterprise-scale reusable capability or platform.
5. Risk / Compliance Complexity

Measures risk exposure. Higher score means lower risk.

ScoreDefinition
0Prohibitive risk. Should not proceed.
1Very high complexity and regulatory risk.
2High complexity but manageable.
3Moderate complexity with standard controls.
4Low complexity and minimal exposure.
5Very low risk or compliance concerns.
Simple Scoring Interpretation
  • 0-10: Low priority (Green) - do not prioritize unless required for compliance or strategic reasons.
  • 11-16: Average priority (Yellow) - low urgency or needs refinement.
  • 17-21: High priority (Orange) - worth considering; may be a good pilot or backlog candidate.
  • 22-25: Critical priority (Red) - high priority; strong candidate for investment and execution.
